# Session journal

Selective low-friction capture during a session. Append one dated line only for
confirmed information likely to change future work. Do not define stable IDs,
store secrets, paste transcripts, or record ordinary Q&A and brainstorming.

Reconcile material entries into the ledgers, conventions, or runbook at the
next update or checkpoint, then reset this file.
